A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From remembering to acting.

I’m a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Chen Change Loy and Prof. Ziwei Liu at MMLab. I also work on embodied AI at SenseTime.

My work connects memory, policy learning, and realistic evaluation—from personal computers to simulated homes and real robots. Previously, I graduated with First Class Honours from CUHK and worked with Prof. Michael R. Lyu on LLM memorization and privacy.

01 MEMORY

Memory for world
action models.

Connecting what a robot remembers to what it does next.

I assist in designing dual memory, co-develop RMBench++, and build the real-robot pipeline for data acquisition, model deployment, and robot control.

A closer look ＋
  • Endogenous memory compresses older history into gist tokens.
  • Exogenous memory records where predictions and new observations disagree.
  • Evaluation combines training, simulation, and real-robot experiments.

With Dr. Zhonghua Wu

02 ACTION

Learning actions
from video.

Taking learned actions to real robots.

I integrated register-token latent actions into π0.5 and ran closed-loop manipulation evaluation in RoboTwin.

A closer look ＋
  • Policy integration connects native latent actions with downstream robot learning.
  • Real-robot experiments cover teleoperation, data formatting, model inference and deployment, and success-rate evaluation.

With Dr. Qingyi Tao
