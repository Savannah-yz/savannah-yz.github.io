01 / ABOUT✳

Intelligence that lives
beyond the screen.

I’m a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Chen Change Loy and Prof. Ziwei Liu at MMLab. I’m also an algorithm intern in Embodied AI at SenseTime, Singapore.

My research asks how agents can use past experience, current observations, and feedback to act reliably over long horizons. I work on both policy learning and realistic evaluation, from personal computers to simulated homes and real robots.

Previously, I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from CUHK, where I worked with Prof. Michael R. Lyu on LLM memorization and privacy.
